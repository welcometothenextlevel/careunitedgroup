About Care United Group

A registered NDIS provider based in Auburn, supporting people with disability and their families across Sydney.

Request a quote or call 0440 127 111

Our story

Care United Group is a team dedicated to reliable, high-quality disability support. We grew from modest beginnings, and every step has strengthened what we believe in: working together, acting with integrity, and real respect for the people we support.

Our approach is simple. We start by understanding your needs, then we provide practical support, from a first conversation to hands-on help every day.

We help with health assistance, home care, daily personal activities and community participation, and our own wheelchair-accessible van helps people get where they need to go.

What we value

Four things shape how we work with every participant, every time.

Experience

You deserve support from people who know the work. Experience means calmer days and more confidence, for you and your family.

Punctuality

We turn up when we say we will. If something changes, we tell you early. Your time matters.

Modern methods

We use modern, practical ways of working, so your support is organised, consistent and easy to follow.

Safety and security

High safety and security standards for every participant, at home and out in the community.
