About Care United Group

We're a disability support provider based in Auburn, working with NDIS participants and their families across Sydney.

Support that starts with the person

Good support begins with understanding. Before anything else, we take the time to learn about your needs, your abilities and how you like to live.

From there we create a support plan that is tailored to you. As your needs change, we adjust it, so your support always fits where you are now.

We help with health assistance, home care, daily personal activities and community participation, at home and around Sydney.

What we value

Three things shape how we work with every participant, every time.

  • Safety
